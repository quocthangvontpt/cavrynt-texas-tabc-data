### For Texas POS and merchant-services sales teams

Cavrynt organizes publicly available Texas TABC application records into a structured CSV for B2B prospecting research.

Use it to research recent license-application activity, filter potential opportunities, and reduce manual data preparation.

**Important:** An application is not a confirmed business opening, and the dataset does not guarantee contact information or sales opportunities.
