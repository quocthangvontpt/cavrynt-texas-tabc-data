# Sources and Limitations

Primary source: https://data.texas.gov/d/mxm5-tdpj

- Source records may contain missing or revised values.
- Pending applications are not issued licenses or confirmed business openings.
- Coverage is limited to selected counties, not all of Texas.
- The product does not include verified phone numbers, emails or opening dates.
- The public sample contains fictional records.
- Source data is publicly available; Cavrynt charges for preparation and delivery, not exclusive ownership of government records.
- Users should independently verify business status before commercial outreach or operational decisions.
